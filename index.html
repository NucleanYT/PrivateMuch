PrivateMuch doesn't store anything you type. Use it in a private window and your browser clears the rest when you close it.

Search DuckDuckGo or type a web address. Nothing is stored here.

How private browsing works

When you close a private window, your browser deletes what that session left behind. It doesn't hide you from the sites and networks your traffic passes through.

Deleted when you close the window

  • Searches you type here
  • Pages you visited
  • Cookies and site data
  • Things you typed into forms

Can still see what you do

  • Websites you visit
  • DuckDuckGo and Duck.ai, which get what you send
  • Your work, school or Wi-Fi network
  • Your internet provider
